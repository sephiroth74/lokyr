# lokyr.dev

The sources of `lokyr.dev`: its home page, `public/`, and the documentation's site, which `cargo xtask site`, or `just site`, builds of the public Markdown into `target/site`, each document a page at its path, `.md` made `.html`.

- `public/`: the home page, one HTML file with its styles inline, the icon, and the y of the title, Playfair Display's, in `lokyr-y.woff2`, the glyph alone and renamed Lokyr Y, as the font's licence asks of a modified version; the licence is `lokyr-y-OFL.txt`. The numbers on the page are those of the latest release's session, in [`docs/bench/RELEASES.md`](../docs/bench/RELEASES.md); the icon is `crates/app/resources/icons/lokyr-jormungandr.svg`. Its JSON-LD, what the search engines read of Lokyr, names the latest release's version and notes: a release updates them with the numbers, and a test of the site fails while they are not the feed's, `public/appcast.xml`'s.
- `og/og.html`: the site's social image, which a link to any page shows where it is shared, drawn into `public/og.png`, 1200 × 630, by `just og-image` in Google Chrome without a window; draw it again as the home's look or words change.
- `style.css`: the documentation's pages, in the home page's style.
- `site.toml`: the site's settings, the project's accounts that every page links.
- `site.js`: the documentation's pages' one script, each of its parts a help that the page does without: the top bar's row of sections scrolled to the current one on a phone, and the table of contents following the reading, the part in view marked and its section's group opened. A page of four sections or more has a table of contents of its second and third levels of headings, beside the column from 1100 px, each section a group that opens on its parts, and a short one of its sections under the title on a narrower screen.
- `search.js`: the search's page, of Pagefind's interface, which a query of `?q=` fills; Pagefind indexes the documents' pages as the site is built, where it is there, on the `PATH` or as `just pagefind` fetches it, of the release that the site pins, into `target/tools`.
- **Search engines**: every page names its canonical address, a folder's `index.html` as its folder, and its description, a document's of its first paragraph of 50 characters or more (or its first list item), cut near 155 characters; the search page and `404.html`, the page that GitHub Pages serves of an address that the site does not hold, are `noindex`. The build writes `sitemap.xml`, the home and every document's page, each of its source's last commit's date, which is why the workflow checks out the whole history; `public/robots.txt` names it, and `public/llms.txt` tells what Lokyr is and links its main pages, for the language models' search. After each deployment, the workflow tells the engines of IndexNow (Bing, Yandex and others) of the pages changed since the push's last commit before it, `cargo xtask site --changed-since <commit>`, every page where the template changed; the key that proves the site ours is `public/<key>.txt`, its name the workflow's `INDEXNOW_KEY`.
- **Checks**: the build fails of a link of the pages, the home's too, to a file or an anchor that the site does not hold.
- **Preview**: `just site-serve` builds the site and serves it on `http://127.0.0.1:8124`. It loads nothing from elsewhere and sets no cookies; its scripts are its own, `site.js`, the search's and the home's.
- **Publish**: each push of `main` builds the site and publishes it on GitHub Pages at `https://lokyr.dev`, of the workflow `.github/workflows/site.yml`, with Pagefind's release for Linux that it pins. `public/appcast.xml`, the updates' feed that `https://lokyr.dev/appcast.xml` serves, is written by `just release` of each release, through `cargo xtask feed publish`, and committed once the release is published.
