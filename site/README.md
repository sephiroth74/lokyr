# lokyr.dev

The sources of `lokyr.dev`: its home page, `public/`, and the documentation's site, which `cargo xtask site`, or `just site`, builds of the public Markdown into `target/site`, each document a page at its path, `.md` made `.html`.

- `public/`: the home page, one HTML file with its styles inline, the icon, and the y of the title, Playfair Display's, in `lokyr-y.woff2`, the glyph alone and renamed Lokyr Y, as the font's licence asks of a modified version; the licence is `lokyr-y-OFL.txt`. The numbers on the page are those of the latest release's session, in [`docs/bench/RELEASES.md`](../docs/bench/RELEASES.md); the icon is `crates/app/resources/icons/lokyr-jormungandr.svg`. Its JSON-LD, what the search engines read of Lokyr, names its screenshots, the home's, each of its image's text as its caption, which a test of the site holds to the home's images, and the latest release's version and notes: a release updates them with the numbers, and a test of the site fails while they are not the feed's, `public/appcast.xml`'s. The road, its steps in a row, a column each, tells their count and the one under way, from 1, in its list's `style`, `--steps` and `--now`, which its line's colors reach: a release that adds a step or moves the one under way changes both, as a test of the site fails while they are not its list's.
- `og/og.html`: the site's social image, which a link to any page shows where it is shared, drawn into `public/og.png`, 1200 × 630, by `just og-image` in Google Chrome without a window; draw it again as the home's look or words change.
- `style.css`: the documentation's pages, in the home page's style.
- `site.toml`: the site's settings, the project's accounts that every page links.
- `../docs/SUMMARY.md`: the documentation's pages in their groups (Start, Use, Reference, Help, Internals) and their order, each group a `## ` heading and each page a list item of a link alone, two spaces deeper under the page that holds it. Every page shows it as its sidebar, at the left from 1100 px, the page shown marked and the pages that hold it open, and as a menu that opens above the page on a narrower screen. The build fails of a public page that the summary leaves out, or of a page that it lists twice or that is not there; the measurements (`docs/bench/`) and the release notes (`docs/releases/`) are covered by the one page of their folder that it lists.
- `site.js`: the documentation's pages' one script, each of its parts a help that the page does without: the top bar's row of sections scrolled to the current one on a phone; the table of contents following the reading, the part in view marked and its section's group opened; and a Copy button on each code block, shown as the pointer comes over it, where the page may write to the clipboard; and the benches' charts of the releases answer the pointer and the keyboard, a release under the pointer, or of the arrow keys once a chart has the focus, marked in every panel of its chart, a card of its values in the panel, each one's change from the release before and its share of the budget, a click or Return opening the release's report, and a legend's item turning its series off and on. A page of four sections or more has a table of contents of its second and third levels of headings, at the right from 1360 px, each section a group that opens on its parts, and a short one of its sections under the title on a narrower screen.
- **Charts**: an image alone in its paragraph of an SVG of `docs/` whose root tells its kind, `data-chart`, as `tools/bench/charts.py` draws the releases' charts, goes into its page as the SVG itself, its image's text its label, in the site's dark colors whatever the reader's appearance, of the page's width; the SVG stays a file of its own for GitHub, which shows it as an image of the reader's appearance. A page of charts is wide: from 1100 px its column grows to 960 px, its charts and its tables taking it, its prose kept to the text's 700. Its tables take the column's width, of alternate rows and tabular figures, their header held under the top bar as the page scrolls and their first column as a table scrolls sideways; the script marks a group's row of its title alone, colors a cell's sign of a verdict, ▲, ▼ or ✖, dims a measure not taken, and marks the column under the pointer, the release of its header in the page's charts with it.
- **Markdown**: GitHub's alerts, `> [!NOTE]`, `> [!TIP]`, `> [!IMPORTANT]`, `> [!WARNING]` and `> [!CAUTION]`, show as callouts of their kind's title and colour, as GitHub shows them. Each page's footer links it in GitHub's editor, "Edit this page on GitHub".
- `search.js`: the search's page, of Pagefind's interface, which a query of `?q=` fills; Pagefind indexes the documents' pages as the site is built, where it is there, on the `PATH` or as `just pagefind` fetches it, of the release that the site pins, into `target/tools`. Pagefind weighs headings above the text of its own; a page's title weighs more still, 10, and a heading of code alone, as a key of the config or a command, 8, as high as a title.
- **Search engines**: every page names its canonical address, a folder's `index.html` as its folder, and its description, a document's of its first paragraph of 50 characters or more (or its first list item), cut near 155 characters; the search page and `404.html`, the page that GitHub Pages serves of an address that the site does not hold, are `noindex`, and so are a report's appendices, the files of its own folder in `docs/bench/`, such as its tables or its machine's setup, which the report stands for. A page's title in the results and in a shared link's preview is its heading's, but where `TITLES` in `tools/xtask/src/site.rs` gives it one that says more, of the words that people search and of 60 characters at most with the site's name; its heading, the sidebar and the breadcrumbs keep the document's own, and the build fails of a title there of no document. Every page has one heading of its title: a document's first, `# `, and the release notes', which have none of their own as GitHub's release shows them, their version's, "Lokyr 1.4.0 release notes". The build writes `sitemap.xml`, the home and every document's page but the appendices, each of its source's last commit's date, which is why the workflow checks out the whole history; `public/robots.txt` names it, and `public/llms.txt` tells what Lokyr is and links its main pages, for the language models' search. Each documentation page carries its breadcrumbs as JSON-LD, a `BreadcrumbList` through the folders of an index of their own; and the build writes `releases.xml`, an Atom feed of the release notes, each dated of its `CHANGELOG.md` heading, which every page's head and footer link. After each deployment, the workflow tells the engines of IndexNow (Bing, Yandex and others) of the pages changed since the push's last commit before it, `cargo xtask site --changed-since <commit>`, every page where the template changed; the key that proves the site ours is `public/<key>.txt`, its name the workflow's `INDEXNOW_KEY`.
- **Checks**: the build fails of a link of the pages, the home's too, to a file or an anchor that the site does not hold; of a document of no title, a `# ` heading first, the release notes aside; and of an image that tells nothing to whom cannot see it: an `<img>` of no `alt`, or of an empty one but in a link of its own words, as the brand's icon beside the name, or marked as a decoration (`aria-hidden="true"`, `role="presentation"`), and an `<svg>` of `role="img"` of no label. An image of an SVG of `docs/` takes the SVG's size, its `width` and `height`, so that the page keeps its room before it loads and does not move as it does.
- **Preview**: `just site-serve` builds the site and serves it on `http://127.0.0.1:8124`. It loads nothing from elsewhere and sets no cookies; its scripts are its own, `site.js`, the search's and the home's.
- **Publish**: each push of `main` builds the site and publishes it on GitHub Pages at `https://lokyr.dev`, of the workflow `.github/workflows/site.yml`, with Pagefind's release for Linux that it pins. `public/appcast.xml`, the updates' feed that `https://lokyr.dev/appcast.xml` serves, is written by `just release` of each release, through `cargo xtask feed publish`, and committed once the release is published.
