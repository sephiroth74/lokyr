# F3's architecture against F2

Measured on 2026-11-02, between 08:20 and 08:32, in one session: the bundle of F2, `main` at d458e4c, which tells `0.1.0-20261101`, against the bundle of F3's 0.2.0, `f3` at 50b3ed8, built with `LOKYR_RELEASE=0.2.0`, each measure in turns, F2 then F3, against the [budgets](../PERFORMANCE.md#budgets). The raw reports, the script and the config are in [ab-F3-13/](ab-F3-13/).

## Method

- **One session, in turns**: the machine's state moves the figures from a day to the next, as [startup-F2-53](startup-F2-53.md) found, so that F2's are measured again beside F3's, rather than compared with the figures of their earlier reports. `run.sh` runs each measure for F2, then for F3, twice; the latency five times.
- **The config**, of its own through `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, as [compare-F2-134](compare-F2-134.md)'s: Menlo at 13 points, no ligatures, a window of 120 × 40 cells, opaque, about 10 000 rows of scrollback; the cursor blinks, as by default.
- **Startup**: `lokyr-bench startup --first-frame`, warm, 10 runs after 2, from the process's start to the first frame on the display; the minimal AppKit app of F0, `Spike.app`, its first window, in the same rounds. The cold startup, which needs `sudo purge`, is left out.
- **Latency**: `lokyr --bench latency --keys 200`, the app's own keys, one every 40 to 62 ms, to their echo on the display.
- **Idle and memory**: the bundle started with `open -n`, its window in front; `lokyr-bench idle` over 60 s, 20 s after the start, once the cursor stopped blinking; `lokyr-bench memory` over 10 s after it.
- **Throughput on screen**: `lokyr --bench output --window 1920x1080` running `tools/bench/onscreen.py` on each workload of [onscreen-F2-56](onscreen-F2-56.md), three times, and the frames' times on the CPU.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2 (26B123), on AC power; the main display a 6016 × 3384 panel at 60 Hz, as 3008 × 1692 points, on which every window opened; a load of about 2.3 from other apps.

- **No budget is worse than F2's beyond its measure's spread.** Startup, idle, memory and throughput are F2's within their runs' spread: the throughputs' runs of each bundle move by 2 to 7%, more than the bundles differ.
- **The latency's p99** of F3's first round, 59.1 ms, is two samples of 200 past 43 ms, which the second round did not have; in rounds 3 to 5, which the machine's load made slower for both, F2's p99 took 67 to 74 ms, and F3's 69 to 71. The p50 moves with F2's in each round.
- **The frames' p99** of the short workloads, 60 to 104 frames, is their slowest frame: 1.3 against 2.2 ms for `seq`, 3.8 against 2.5 for SGR, 1.6 against 3.1 for CJK, whose frames draw glyphs that the atlas lacks; one frame each way, not a trend.
- **At 60 Hz**: the main display runs at 60 Hz, where [latency-F2-54](latency-F2-54.md) measured the budget's 120 Hz on the built-in display; the frames' counts, 1.1 to 1.2 at the p50 and 2.6 to 3.6 at the p99 in the first rounds, are within the budget of 2 and 4.5 frames at either rate.
