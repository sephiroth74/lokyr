# 0.8.0 against 0.7.0 (F4-25)

Measured on 2026-11-09 in one session, between 15:51 and 16:13, the user away: the app of 0.7.0's DMG, of commit 603c6ab, against the app of 0.8.0's, of commit 2cf4c326, both notarized and stapled, as `just release` built them, each started once before the measured rounds; then the window's motion, until 17:00; then a second session of the latency alone, until 17:08. The reports are in [ab-F4-25](ab-F4-25/), with the session's script, [`run.sh`](ab-F4-25/run.sh), the plugins' homes', [`plugins.sh`](ab-F4-25/plugins.sh), its analysis, [`analyze.py`](ab-F4-25/analyze.py), and its tables, [`analysis.txt`](ab-F4-25/analysis.txt); the second latency session's in [latency](ab-F4-25/latency/).

## Method

As [F3-77's](ab-F3-77.md#method): F2-134's config, Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, an opaque window, of balanced orders: the startup, warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, four rounds, AB BA BA AB, each of the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; the latency of 200 keys, eight rounds, after a warm-up of 20 keys of each bundle; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds; the throughput of F2-56's four workloads on screen, two runs in each of two rounds; the same pause of 2 s before each run of the startup and of the latency. Each copy, new out of its DMG, started once before any measure, as [F3-228](startup-F3-228.md) found a new executable's first starts slowed by the scans of Microsoft Defender.

Besides, of 0.8.0 alone:
- **The footprint of Luau's plugins**: the app started of a home of its own, `HOME` and `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, of no plugin, and of one of ten plugins of the template that `sleip new` makes, each of its own name and namespace and of no `[activation]`, so that it activates as the app starts, installed by 0.8.0's Sleip, `sleip add --path`; idle, memory and the process's threads as above, two rounds, the order reversed in the second.
- **A plugin's activation**, its compilation within, and its VM's heap: `just bench-luau`, a release build's activation of a fresh plugin on the SDK's fake host, its VM made, its sources compiled and its `activate` run, of the template, 67 lines, and of a plugin of 2017 lines, 200 functions of a loop, which registers ten commands, in both executions, 50 runs each after the process's first.
- **The binary's size**: the bundle's and the DMG's, and the app's code by its symbols' addresses, as `nm` gives no sizes of Mach-O, Luau's C++, mlua's and `plugin-host-luau`'s apart.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2, on AC power, the main display a Dell P2721Q at 60 Hz, a scale of 2.
- The load average 1.4 to 5.5 through the session, of no other work but the system's, Jamf's and Microsoft Defender's; 7.4 down to 2.6 through the second latency session, as Defender scanned the window's motion's recordings, several GB, about 60 % of a core.
