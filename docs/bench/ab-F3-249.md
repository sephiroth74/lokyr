# The inline images' cost of none shown

Measured on 2026-11-12 between 15:15 and 15:23, unattended: the app of 0.9.0's release, the copy that `/Applications` held, against the bundle of the inline images, of `just bundle` of the branch that brings them, both copied anew into `/tmp/lokyr` and started once before any measure, as a scan of a new executable slows its first starts. The reports are in [ab-F3-249](ab-F3-249/), with the session's script, [`run.sh`](ab-F3-249/run.sh), its analysis, [`analyze.py`](ab-F3-249/analyze.py), and its tables, [`analysis.txt`](ab-F3-249/analysis.txt).

## Method

As in [0.9.0's A/B](ab-F4-42.md#method), of its parts that the images could touch: the config of [compare-F2-134](compare-F2-134.md), Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, an opaque window; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds, the order reversed in the second; the throughput of the four workloads of [onscreen-F2-56](onscreen-F2-56.md) on screen, two runs in each of two rounds. No image shows in any run: the measures tell what the images' code costs where no program sends one, a snapshot's look at the placements of none and a parser's limit of OSC 1337. The startup and the latency, whose code the images leave as it was, are not measured.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2, on AC power, the main display a Dell P2721Q at 60 Hz, a scale of 2.
- The load average 1.2 to 4.4 through the session, of no other work but the system's, Jamf's and Microsoft Defender's.
