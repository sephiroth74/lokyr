# 0.9.0 against 0.8.0

Measured on 2026-11-11 in one session, between 22:55 and 23:17, unattended: the app of 0.8.0's DMG, of commit 2cf4c326, notarized and stapled, the copy of 0.8.0's session, against 0.9.0's, of commit 43167671, the bundle of `just bundle` signed with the Developer ID and not notarized, as `just release` releases a commit that GitHub has, which waited for a push; its binaries are those that the release signs, and notarizing changes nothing of how they run. The reports are in [ab-F4-42](ab-F4-42/), with the session's script, [`run.sh`](ab-F4-42/run.sh), the plugins' homes', [`plugins.sh`](ab-F4-42/plugins.sh), its analysis, [`analyze.py`](ab-F4-42/analyze.py), and its tables, [`analysis.txt`](ab-F4-42/analysis.txt).

## Method

As in [0.8.0's A/B](ab-F4-25.md#method): the config of [compare-F2-134](compare-F2-134.md), Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, an opaque window, of balanced orders: the startup, warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, four rounds, AB BA BA AB, each of the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; the latency of 200 keys, eight rounds, after a warm-up of 20 keys of each bundle; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds; the throughput of the four workloads of [onscreen-F2-56](onscreen-F2-56.md) on screen, two runs in each of two rounds; the same pause of 2 s before each run of the startup and of the latency. Each copy started once before any measure, 0.9.0's new out of its build.

Besides, of 0.9.0 alone:
- **The published plugins' footprint**: the app started of a home of its own, `HOME` and `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, of no plugin, and of one of `sephiroth74/snap` 0.1.0 and `sephiroth74/awake` 0.1.0, installed from pkg.lokyr.dev by 0.9.0's Sleip, `sleip add`, their capabilities granted in the home's `permissions.toml`, so that no question waits at the start: `awake` activates as the app starts, of its button in the titlebar, and `snap` as its command first runs, installed and asleep; idle, memory and the process's threads as above, two rounds, the order reversed in the second.
- **A plugin's activation**: `just bench-luau`, as in 0.8.0's A/B, on the machine at rest after the session.
- **The binary's size**: the bundle's, its two binaries', and a DMG of the bundle made as `just dmg` makes it, of its format `ULFO`, not notarized.

The window's motion, which Instruments records while `lokyr-bench motion` drags and resizes the window by the mouse's synthetic events, did not run in the session: the events take the Accessibility permission of the app that runs the bench, which the session's had not. It ran later, on 12 November 2026 between 00:14 and 01:28, from a terminal that had the permission, [below](#the-windows-motion).

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2, on AC power, the main display a Dell P2721Q at 60 Hz, a scale of 2.
- The load average 1.4 to 3.1 through the session, of no other work but the system's, Jamf's and Microsoft Defender's.

## The window's motion
