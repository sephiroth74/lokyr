# 0.7.0 against 0.5.1

Measured on 2026-11-08 in one session, between 16:28 and 16:43, unattended: the app of 0.5.1's DMG, of commit 30e2d1e, against the app of 0.7.0's, of commit 6ba2c80, both notarized and stapled, as `just release` built them; then the window's motion, until 17:00; then a second session of the startup alone, at 17:11. The reports are in [ab-F3-77](ab-F3-77/), with the session's script, [`run.sh`](ab-F3-77/run.sh), its analysis, [`analyze.py`](ab-F3-77/analyze.py), and its tables, [`analysis.txt`](ab-F3-77/analysis.txt); the startup's second session in [startup-F3-227](startup-F3-227/).

0.7.0's first draft, of 2691137, was measured the same way between 14:56 and 15:25: its window's motion moved nothing, as a start from a shell opened its window a second late and behind the app in front, which a fix took away before the draft was published; its other measures were these within their spread. This report is of the second draft, the release.

## Method

As in [0.5.1's A/B](ab-F3-190.md#method): the config of [compare-F2-134](compare-F2-134.md), Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, an opaque window, of balanced orders: the startup, warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, four rounds, AB BA BA AB, each of the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; the latency of 200 keys, eight rounds, after a warm-up of 20 keys of each bundle; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds; the throughput of the four workloads of [onscreen-F2-56](onscreen-F2-56.md) on screen, two runs in each of two rounds; the same pause of 2 s before each run of the startup and of the latency.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2, on AC power, the main display a Dell P2721Q at 60 Hz, a scale of 2.
- The load average 2.2 to 3.9 through the session, of no other work but the system's and Microsoft Defender's, whose real-time protection scans the executables that start, the new ones the longest; about 7 through the startup's second session, after the window's motion.

- **Every budget holds but the startup's gap in this session, which holds at rest**: [startup-F3-228](startup-F3-228.md) measured it again, each copy started before, 18.8 ms against 0.5.1's 15.1. The latency, idle, the memory, the throughput and the frames are 0.5.1's within their spread: the latency's p50 0.6 ms longer, within its rounds' standard deviation of 0.9 ms; its p99 5.6 ms longer, within 0.7.0's standard deviation of 7.9 ms, 7 samples in the fourth refresh against 1; the footprint 0.7 MB larger than 0.5.1's second round, its first 29.8 MB; the throughputs 0 to 4 % higher.
- **The startup is 30 ms longer in the session**, the medians of its rounds 266.5 ms against 236.3, 19 to 36 ms a round, its gap 46.9 ms against 16.6, past the budget of 35, in each round, the last the least, 19 ms. The startup's second session, half an hour later, found 12 ms, the gap 35.2 ms against 23.4, the machine loaded. The first draft's sessions told the same story: 20 ms longer in its A/B, its copy freshly out of its DMG too, and 0.8 ms in a second session after it had started about a hundred times, its gap 28.8 ms against 27.9. What the release adds to the start is small: the restore's look at the system's setting, the services' provider, and the default terminal's look, which the first draft's fix moved to a thread of its own. Microsoft Defender's scans of a new executable, which the copies of 0.5.1, started for days, no longer take, fit the sessions' story; the thread of the default terminal's look, which starts with the app, is the other lead. Measured again at rest, of copies started before and of the thread's look put off, in [startup-F3-228](startup-F3-228.md): 0.7.0's gap 18.8 ms against 0.5.1's 15.1, within the budget, the thread taking nothing, so that the session's 30 ms were the antivirus's scans of a new copy.

## The window's motion

Measured between 16:43 and 17:00, of [motion/run.sh](ab-F3-77/motion/run.sh) and its reports in [motion](ab-F3-77/motion/), as in [0.5.1's A/B](ab-F3-190.md#the-windows-motion): each window dragged by its titlebar, then resized by its corner, 10 s each along a sine of 200 points, while Instruments' Animation Hitches recorded every process, opaque, and translucent at 0.95 with a blur of 10; two rounds, the second in the reverse order. The traces, too large to keep, gave [`figures.csv`](ab-F3-77/motion/figures.csv).

The means of the two rounds; the missed refreshes of 600; the resize's CPUs.

- **The drag and the live resize show as many frames as 0.5.1's**, 58 a second of a drag, 56 of a resize, of as many missed refreshes and the same CPU. The resize's delay at the 95th percentile is either about 13 ms or about 28 ms in a run, a refresh apart, in both apps, as 0.5.1's A/B found.
- The first draft's window, started from a shell, opened behind the app in front, and the motion's events moved the app in front instead: fixed before the release, its drag 206 points and its resize 114, as 0.5.1's.
