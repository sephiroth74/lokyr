# 0.5.0 against 0.4.0

Measured on 2026-11-05 in two sessions, between 14:01 and 14:14 and between 14:16 and 14:31: the app of 0.4.0's DMG, of commit ba5508c, against the app of 0.5.0's DMG, of commit 15fca01, both notarized and stapled. The reports are in [ab-F3-40](ab-F3-40/), the second session's in [second](ab-F3-40/second/), with the sessions' scripts, `run.sh` and `second.sh`, their config and `analyze.py`, which writes the tables of a session's reports.

## Method

As in [0.4.0's A/B](ab-F3-29.md#method), the config of [compare-F2-134](compare-F2-134.md), Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, but of balanced orders, as [latency-F3-135](latency-F3-135.md) found the place in a round to move the latency's tail more than any bundle: each measure's rounds put each bundle first as often.

- **The first session**: the startup, warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, two rounds, AB and BA, each of the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; the latency of 200 keys, eight rounds, AB BA BA AB AB BA BA AB, after a warm-up of 20 keys of each; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds, AB and BA; the throughput of the four workloads of [onscreen-F2-56](onscreen-F2-56.md) on screen, two runs in each of two rounds, AB and BA.
- **The second session**, as the first left the latency's tail undecided, of a load average of 4 to 6.5: the startup in four rounds, AB BA BA AB, and the latency in eight, as the first's, the load average told after each round.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2 (26B123), on AC power; the main display, and the only one, a Dell P2721Q at 60 Hz, of 5120 × 2880 pixels at a scale of 2, on which every window opened. 0.4.0's A/B ran on a Dell U3417W at a scale of 1, so the GPU's memory and a frame's pixels compare within these sessions only.
- The load average was 4.0 to 6.6 in the first session, of other work on the machine, and 1.7 to 5.1 in the second, 2 to 3 in most of its rounds; each measure's balanced orders share it between the bundles.

- **The latency's tail is 0.5.0's a few samples longer**, in both sessions, as [latency-F3-163](latency-F3-163.md) then bisected: its p99 3.6 ms higher over the 16 rounds, about 2.4 times the difference's standard error, of 25 samples of 3184 in the fourth refresh or later against 15, 0.8% against 0.5%. The place in a round moves it no more here, 49.2 and 50.2 ms in the first session, 47.9 and 47.6 ms in the second, the first place's and the second's, so that the difference is the bundles'. The medians are the same; the tail stays at about 3 frames at 60 Hz, as 0.4.0's.
- **The startup takes a refresh more, now and then**: 0.5.0's runs come on the second step of a refresh at 60 Hz, about 237 ms, in each of the second session's rounds, 0.4.0's on the first step, 221 and 230 ms, in two of the four. Both are within the budget. The first session's startup, of a floor that moved from 214 to 187 ms between its two rounds under its load, compares nothing.
- **Idle, memory and throughput are 0.4.0's** within their runs' spread. 0.5.0's footprint without the GPU's is 0.4 MB larger in both rounds, as its binary is 0.98 MB larger, 8.29 MB against 7.31; one wakeup of a package's idle in one round of 60 s.
