# 1.0.0 against 0.9.0

Measured on 2026-11-13 in one session, between 11:15 and 11:31, unattended: the app of 0.9.0's DMG, of commit db3afe17, notarized and stapled, against 1.0.0's, of its candidate's DMG, 1.0.0-rc.1, of commit 118d38e7, notarized and stapled, whose code 1.0.0 releases. The reports are in [ab-F3-85](ab-F3-85/), with the session's script, [`run.sh`](ab-F3-85/run.sh), its analysis, [`analyze.py`](ab-F3-85/analyze.py), and its tables, [`analysis.txt`](ab-F3-85/analysis.txt).

## Method

As in [0.9.0's A/B](ab-F4-42.md#method): the config of [compare-F2-134](compare-F2-134.md), Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, an opaque window, of balanced orders: the startup, warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, four rounds, AB BA BA AB, each of the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; the latency of 200 keys, eight rounds, after a warm-up of 20 keys of each bundle; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds; the throughput of the four workloads of [onscreen-F2-56](onscreen-F2-56.md) on screen, two runs in each of two rounds; the same pause of 2 s before each run of the startup and of the latency. Each copy, new out of its DMG, started once before any measure.

The window's motion did not run: `lokyr-bench motion` takes the Accessibility permission of the app that runs it, which the session's had not.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2.1, on AC power, the main display a Dell P2721Q at 60 Hz, a scale of 2.
- The load average 1.7 to 5.3 through the session, of no other work but the system's, Jamf's and Microsoft Defender's.

- **Every budget holds, and no measure is worse beyond its spread.** The latency's p50 is 0.03 ms longer and its p99 1.1 ms longer, within the rounds' standard deviations of 0.9 and 7.3 ms, the place in a round moving the p99 more than the bundle, 49.5 ms first against 46.9 second; 11 samples in the fourth refresh against 9. The throughputs are within 5 %, `seq` the faster by 4.8 %, and the frames within their rounds.
- **The startup's gap is 2.1 ms shorter**, 26.7 ms against 28.9: 13.5 ms in the first round, then 32.7, 32.1 and 28.6, 0.9.0's 26.5, 30.3, 27.3 and 31.2, the rounds' spread wider than the difference.

- **The interrupt wakeups are 0.35 a second** in both rounds, 0.9.0's 0.35 and 0.33, within the tolerance of 0.05.
