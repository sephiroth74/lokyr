# The build of 6 November against 0.5.0, and the single tab's title

Measured on 2026-11-06 in one session, between 22:24 and 22:41, unattended: the app of 0.5.0's DMG, of commit b8f8103, notarized and stapled, against the app of the build of commit a3e5b0b, `0.5.0-20261106`, signed, of `just bundle`; then that build with the tabs plugin and without it, `[plugins.tabs] enabled = false`, to tell the cost of a single tab's title, which the plugin draws in the titlebar since that build. The reports are in [ab-F3-49](ab-F3-49/), with the session's script, `run.sh`, its config, `analyze.py` and its tables, `analysis.txt`.

## Method

As in [0.5.0's A/B](ab-F3-40.md#method), the config of [compare-F2-134](compare-F2-134.md), Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, an opaque window, of balanced orders: the startup, warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, four rounds, AB BA BA AB, each of the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; the latency of 200 keys, eight rounds, after a warm-up of 20 keys of each bundle; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds; the throughput of the four workloads of [onscreen-F2-56](onscreen-F2-56.md) on screen, two rounds of two runs each. Then, of the build of now with the tabs plugin (`cur`) and without it (`notabs`): the startup in two rounds, the latency in four, and the frames of a program that sets the window's title 100 times, 20 a second, in two rounds; and the frames of a program that tells its progress, OSC 9;4, 50 times, 10 a second, of a single tab and of a bar shown, `hide-when-single = false`, two runs each.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2, on AC power, the main display at 60 Hz, a scale of 2, as in 0.5.0's A/B.
- The load average 1.0 to 2.8 through the session, unattended, of no other work but the system's.

## The start's cause and its fix, and the latency again

Measured on 2026-11-07, between 00:56 and 01:06, unattended, of [f3-187/fix.sh](ab-F3-49/f3-187/fix.sh) and [f3-187/fixlat.sh](ab-F3-49/f3-187/fixlat.sh), their reports and [analysis.txt](ab-F3-49/f3-187/analysis.txt) in [f3-187](ab-F3-49/f3-187/): the build of a3e5b0b (`cur`) against the build of the fix with the tabs plugin (`fix`) and without it (`fixnotabs`), the startup to the first frame and to the window on screen, of `lokyr-bench startup`, in four rounds of balanced orders, then the latency in four; the load average 1.9 to 7.0, of the system's own work.

| Measure | Before the fix | The fix | The fix, without the tabs plugin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Startup, first frame, median of each round | 249.3, 249.0, 247.9 and 236.7 ms | 233.3, 234.0, 234.8 and 233.3 ms | 233.2, 232.1, 241.4 and 236.8 ms |
| Their mean | 245.7 ms | 233.8 ms | 235.8 ms |
| Startup, the window on screen, their mean | 250.2 ms | 227.3 ms | 224.6 ms |
| Latency, p50, the mean of 4 rounds | 24.14 ms | 23.80 ms | 26.65 ms |
| Latency, p99, the mean of 4 rounds | 41.05 ms | 42.16 ms | 72.24 ms |
| Its samples in the fourth refresh or later, of 796 | 1 | 0 | 76 |

- **The cause of the start's refresh**: the first frame drew the slots' views before it looked at the tab's place, its folder and its program. The window's opening looked at it first, but gave up on a folder that cannot be read, as that of `login`, which runs as root, where the frame's own look, after the views, keeps the folder before and takes the program: the tab's label changed within the first frame, after the title's view had drawn it, and the `tab-changed` of the turn's end had the tabs plugin draw its view again, a frame a refresh after the first, as AppKit made the window show. A frame then delays the window's showing by a refresh: a trace of the frames, of the main thread's run loop and of the window's steps from its `makeKeyAndOrderFront` found the frames before the window's showing skipped by the display in both builds, the window showing about 50 ms after its order, and the first frame shown 94 ms after it of the title against 79 without, of the same times before it. The window's opening now looks at the place as the frame does, so that the first frame draws the label that stays, and no second frame comes.
- **Not the cause**, as experimental builds of the trace measured, in balanced orders: the titlebar of no title set before the window shows, the view's areas of the pointer's moves, the interface's face laid out before, the window shown after the frame of the title, which takes as long, its order later by as much, and the first frame drawn at the turn's end, which shows a refresh later of both builds.
- **The fix starts as the build without the tabs plugin does**, 233.8 ms against 235.8, and its window shows 23 ms sooner than before it, within 3 ms of the build without the plugin.
- **The latency's cost did not repeat**: of the tabs plugin, before the fix and after it, the median is 24.1 and 23.8 ms, against 26.7 ms without the plugin, which the first session measured 2.7 ms shorter; the tail is shorter with the plugin, as in the first session, p99 41 to 42 ms against 72, of one sample in the fourth refresh or later against 76. The tabs plugin hears no event and asks no frame while the keys are typed, the frames are as many, and a frame's CPU is 0.12 ms longer of the title's view: the medians move between sessions by more than the plugin, and the shorter tail of the title's view is not explained, a gain. The session's latencies are longer than the first session's, every build alike, of its load.
