# The build after F3-49 against 0.5.0, and the single tab's title (after F3-49)

Measured on 2026-11-06 in one session, between 22:24 and 22:41, the user away: the app of 0.5.0's DMG, of commit 15fca01, notarized and stapled, against the app of the build of commit 222aa8c, `0.5.0-20261106`, signed, of `just bundle`; then that build with the tabs plugin and without it, `[plugins.tabs] enabled = false`, to tell the cost of a single tab's title, which the plugin draws in the titlebar since F3-177. The reports are in [ab-F3-49](ab-F3-49/), with the session's script, `run.sh`, its config, `analyze.py` and its tables, `analysis.txt`.

## Method

As [F3-40](ab-F3-40.md#method)'s, F2-134's config, Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells, an opaque window, of balanced orders: the startup, warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, four rounds, AB BA BA AB, each of the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; the latency of 200 keys, eight rounds, after a warm-up of 20 keys of each bundle; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start, two rounds; the throughput of F2-56's four workloads on screen, two rounds of two runs each. Then, of the build of now with the tabs plugin (`cur`) and without it (`notabs`): the startup in two rounds, the latency in four, and the frames of a program that sets the window's title 100 times, 20 a second, in two rounds; and the frames of a program that tells its progress, OSC 9;4, 50 times, 10 a second, of a single tab and of a bar shown, `hide-when-single = false`, two runs each.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2, on AC power, the main display at 60 Hz, a scale of 2, as F3-40's.
- The load average 1.0 to 2.8 through the session, the user away, of no other work but the system's.

- **The tabs plugin's title of a single tab costs a refresh at the start and 2.7 ms of the median latency**: without the plugin, the build of now starts in 216 ms against 233, a refresh at 60 Hz, and echoes a key in 18.7 ms at the median against 21.5, more of its samples in the first refresh, 79 of 199 against 37. 0.5.0, whose tabs plugin draws nothing of a single tab, starts and echoes as the build of now does without it. The budgets hold, but the title is the cost that the user asked to be told first, and the first to remove if the speed is lost ([F3-177](../tasks/F3-macos-mvp.md#f3-177--tabs-a-spinner-of-the-programs-that-tell-no-progress-and-the-single-tabs-title)).
- **The latency's tail is shorter with the title**, p99 35 ms against 47 to 51, of no sample in the fourth refresh, in both comparisons: the frames come at a steadier place in the refresh, which the measures do not explain yet.
- **Each title's change draws a frame**, 102 frames of 100 changes against one without the plugin, of about 0.56 ms of the CPU each, as F3-177's notes told.
- **A program's progress draws no frame of its own**: of a bar shown, 50 changes draw 10 or 11 frames, the window server sliding the fills, as [F3-144](../tasks/F3-macos-mvp.md#f3-144--a-progress-of-a-nodes-outline-and-its-values-slide-by-the-window-server) meant; of a single tab, the progress shows nowhere, as the title shows a ring that stands still, which is to fix.
- **A frame of CJK and emoji takes 0.26 ms more on the CPU**, at the median, and the frames' p99 of `seq`, SGR and CJK about 1 to 3 ms more, of the same throughput; ASCII's throughput is 2.6% lower in all four runs. Not explained yet: a single pane, no fade; the title's view drawn in each frame is the first to look at.
- **Idle and memory are 0.5.0's**, the footprint 0.9 MB larger.
