# 0.3.0 against 0.2.0 (F3-83)

Measured on 2026-11-02, between 15:58 and 16:08, in one session: the bundle of 0.2.0, of its tag `v0.2.0`, built with `LOKYR_RELEASE=0.2.0`, against the app of 0.3.0's DMG, of commit 27a0762, notarized and stapled, each measure in turns, as [F3-13](ab-F3-13.md) measured 0.2.0 against F2. The reports are in [ab-F3-83](ab-F3-83/), with the session's script, `run.sh`, its config and `analyze.py`, which writes the tables of the reports.

## Method

As [F3-13](ab-F3-13.md#method)'s: one session, in turns, twice; F2-134's config, Menlo at 13 points, 120 × 40 cells; startup warm, 10 runs after 2, to the first frame on the display, and the minimal AppKit app of F0 as its floor; latency of 200 keys; idle over 60 s and memory over 10 s of a bundle started with `open -n`, 20 s after its start; the throughput of F2-56's four workloads on screen, three runs each.

0.3.0 loads Sparkle's updater once its first window opens, out of the main thread, which the idle and memory's measures, started with `open`, hold; the benches of startup, latency and throughput run without it, as their checks would reach the network.

## Machine

- Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2 (26B123), on AC power; the main display a 6016 × 3384 panel at 60 Hz, as 3008 × 1692 points, on which every window opened.

- **No budget is worse beyond its measure's spread.** Latency, idle, memory and throughput are 0.2.0's within their runs' spread: the throughputs' runs of each bundle move by 3 to 20%, more than the bundles differ.
- **Sparkle at rest**: the updater, loaded and started in 0.3.0, adds no wakeup, and its memory, under 0.5 MB, is within the footprint's spread.
- **The startup takes a refresh more.** The runs of both fall on steps of a refresh at 60 Hz, 16.7 ms apart, about 219, 236, 252 and 269 ms: 0.2.0's mostly on the first, 0.3.0's mostly on the second, in both rounds. The work before the first frame grew by enough to miss a refresh, a few milliseconds; the budget, 35 ms over the minimal app, holds at 14 ms, and the difference, 7%, is within the startup's spread of 8 to 20%. [F3-105](../tasks/F3-macos-mvp.md#f3-105--the-first-frame-a-refresh-later-than-020s) found it, a second frame at once that the plugins' turn asked for, and fixed it ([startup-F3-105](startup-F3-105.md)).
- **The latency's p99** of 0.3.0's first round, 57.8 ms, is a few samples of 200 past 44 ms, which the second round did not have, where 0.3.0's p99 was 0.2.0's; F3-13's first round had the same.
