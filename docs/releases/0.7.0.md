## What's new

- **Themes**: Lokyr holds about 280 themes of the iTerm2-Color-Schemes collection, a light one and a dark one in force, as `[theme]` names them in the config, which follow macOS's appearance. View › Choose Theme… previews each as you move through them. Double-click an `.itermcolors` file, or drop it on Lokyr's icon in the Dock, to install it; your own themes go in `~/.config/lokyr/themes/`, and `minimum-contrast` keeps text readable on any of them.
- **The command palette**: ⇧⌘P finds any command by its name, and runs it.
- **Shell integration** for zsh, bash and fish, without touching your files: ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ jump between prompts, ⇧⌘↑ and ⇧⌘↓ by line; a bar beside each prompt tells whether its command succeeded; the command whose output you read stays at the pane's top as it scrolls, a click on it going back to its prompt; and a notification tells when a command of 10 seconds or more ends while you look elsewhere, as `[notifications] long-command` says.
- **Copy mode**: ⇧⌘C moves a cursor through the screen and the scrollback with vi's keys; `v`, `V` and ⌃V select characters, lines or a block, and `y` copies.
- **A scroll bar** over each pane, as macOS shows its own, with the find bar's matches marked along it.
- **Programs' progress**, as build tools and Claude Code tell it, in a thin bar across the window's top, besides round its tab.
- **Windows that open again** as Lokyr starts, each where it was, with its tabs, splits and folders, as macOS's "Close windows when quitting an application" setting says, or as `[app] restore-windows` decides.
- **From the Finder**: New Lokyr Window Here and New Lokyr Tab Here on a folder, and a folder dropped on Lokyr's icon in the Dock opens a window there.
- **The default terminal**: Lokyr › Make Lokyr the Default Terminal opens your `.command` scripts, `ssh://` links and man pages' links in Lokyr.
- **Window › Float on Top** keeps a window above the other apps'.
- **A tab's color** from its context menu, of macOS's colors.
- **Translucent windows** show the desktop through the tab bar too, of clean corners.
- **The cursor's movement**: `movement = "snappy"` under `[cursor]` slides the cursor to its new place.
- **Light and dark for programs**: editors such as Neovim and Helix learn whether the theme is light or dark, and follow its changes.
- **More in the menus**: Split Left and Split Up, Close Pane, Copy Link Address, New Window and New Tab at Folder, Search man Page Index, and Search the Web, of `[links] search-engine`.

## Works better

- The find bar keeps its matches through a resize and as the scrollback drops old lines, highlights wide characters whole, and follows text that a program rewrites in place.
- A find bar or the copy mode in a tab that you leave gives the keyboard back to the tab that you go to.
- The bell of a window whose tab bar sits in the titlebar marks the right tab.
- bash's integration works under `set -u`, and keeps its own `$_` and DEBUG trap.

## Before you update

- The quick terminal's settings move from `[quick-terminal]` to `[plugins.quick-terminal]`: Lokyr tells you of an old `[quick-terminal]` section, and ignores it.
- ⇧⌘C starts the copy mode.
