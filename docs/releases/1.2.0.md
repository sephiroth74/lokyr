Themes made in Lokyr: a Theme Editor of every color of a theme, its values edited of a field or of the system's color panel and written into the theme's file, its comments kept; themes made, duplicated, converted and imported in the app; and the settings' Keys page.

## What's new since 1.1.0

- **The Theme Editor**: on the settings window's Themes page, the Editor shows every color of a theme, the terminal's and the interface's by groups, each of its value and where it comes from: the theme's file and its line, a theme that it extends, or a rule. A click on a color opens a field of its value, a color, or for an interface color an expression of other colors, as `accent / 40%`, written into the theme's file as you pause or press Return; a click on its swatch opens the system's color panel, the eyedropper too, the color showing at once as you move. Reset puts a color back as it was when the Editor showed the theme.
- **Previews of the theme edited**: beside the colors, a window of the theme, its tab bar, a scroll bar, a second pane and a terminal of every combination that it colors, and a plugin's panel of real controls, fields, toggles, a segmented control, a list, progresses and buttons, which take the pointer. A click on a part marks the colors that it takes, and a color's click the parts that take it. Your terminals keep their theme while the Editor shows another, and follow your edits of the theme that they use.
- **Themes made in the app**: New… on the Themes page makes a theme of Lokyr's own light or dark values, every key written; Duplicate… in a card's menu copies a theme; View › New Theme from Current… copies the theme in use; View › Import Themes… imports `.itermcolors` and `.toml` themes, several at once. A theme of the collection or a built-in one is read-only in the Editor, whose lock makes a copy of it; an `.itermcolors` theme is converted to Lokyr's format as you edit it, its original moved to the Trash.
- **The interface's colors follow the theme**: a theme's accent colors the focus ring, the selected rows, the toggles, the progress and the gutter of running commands, and its text the fainter texts, unless it gives them too; a color can be an expression of others, as `mix(background, foreground, 8%)`. A theme of the terminal's colors alone looks as it did.
- **A light theme for both appearances**: a click on a theme of the appearance not in force asks whether to use it for its appearance alone or for both.
- **The cursor guide**, a band across the cursor's row, as iTerm2's: View › Show Cursor Guide, ⌥⌘;, or `[cursor] guide`.
- **Faint text** of its own opacity, `[text] faint-opacity`, and an **HDR cursor**, brighter than white on a display of extended dynamic range, `[cursor] hdr`, off by default.
- **The Keys page**: the settings' shortcuts on a page of their own, of a field that filters them by their commands, their keys or their contexts.
- **Window › Cycle Through Windows**, ⌘`, and ⇧⌘` the other way, the settings window among them.
- **Plugins**: the plugin API 0.1.4 reads every color of a theme as it resolves, shows the system's color panel, asks a question of buttons, and draws a part of a view in a theme of a name.

## Changed

- The settings window has a theme of its own, Lokyr Light or Lokyr Dark by the appearance, whatever theme your terminals use.
- What a theme colors nothing with no longer refuses it: a value that does not read, a name of no color or colors that name each other are told in the card, of their line, the rest of the theme in force.
- A plugin's question names the plugin in its alert, unless it is one of Lokyr's own, and a plugin asks one question at a time.
- The settings window draws anew faster, the Keys page above all, and Lokyr starts a little sooner.

## Fixed

- A saved theme that changes the interface's colors alone colors the tab bar, the panels and the settings window at once.
- A block cursor on the cursor guide's row keeps its own color.
- A resize no longer clears a running command's output of a shell whose prompt marks its start alone.
- A pane that takes the keyboard, as Go to Next Agent brings it forward, ends its tab's zoom of another.
- A right click or a ⌘-click on a path of a volume that does not answer, as a network's, no longer holds Lokyr.
- A binding of a character that the US layout types with Shift, as `cmd+}`, runs as its keys are pressed and takes the place of a default of them; two entries of the same keys written two ways read, the later holding, where the whole config was refused.
- `sleip check` refuses a plugin's config schema larger than 64 KiB, the most that Lokyr reads of one.

## Before you update

- Plugins installed for 1.1.0 keep working: the plugin API is 0.1.4, which takes 0.1.3's plugins.
- Your settings window now shows Lokyr's own colors rather than your theme's.
