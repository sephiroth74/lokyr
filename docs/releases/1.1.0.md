Lokyr's settings, in a window of their own: every key of the config, the keys' bindings, the themes and the plugins, changed without editing a file, the config file staying yours, its comments kept.

## What's new since 1.0.0

- **The settings window**: Lokyr › Open Settings Window, or the command palette, opens the settings in a window of their own, of the size of System Settings', its pages as tabs across the top: Settings, Themes and Plugins. Settings…, ⌘,, still opens your config file.
- **Every key of the config**: the Settings page lists every key of the config and of the bundled plugins' sections, by section in a sidebar, each of what it does, its value and a More info link to its text in the config guide. A toggle, a menu or a field changes a key at once, written into your `config.toml` as typing pauses, its comments kept; Reset takes a key back to its default, and a value that a key does not take says why. A search field finds the keys, the sections and the bindings by their words.
- **Fonts, previewed**: a font's family and its styles are picked from menus of the system's fonts, the panes showing each one as its item rests highlighted, until the menu closes; a family chosen comments out the styles of your config that it lacks.
- **Your shortcuts**: the Keys part lists every binding, Lokyr's defaults and yours, by category. A click on a binding's keys records the shortcut that you press next; Remove and Restore take a default away and give it back; Add records a new shortcut and offers every command by category. A shortcut that another binding takes says which, and macOS's own, as ⌘Q, stay as they are.
- **Themes, at a glance**: the Themes page shows every theme, the built-in ones, the collection's and yours, as a sample terminal in its colors, the light and the dark ones apart; a click uses one for its appearance.
- **Plugins, without the command line**: Lokyr › Plugins… lists every plugin, with its state and its capabilities, to disable, enable, update or remove; and the registry's plugins, to install without restarting.
- **View › Open Themes Folder** opens the folder of your own themes in the Finder, making it first if it is not there.
- **Plugins**: the plugin API 0.1.3 lets a plugin show a notice in Lokyr's card, of an icon of its level, as info, success, warning or error, and show a theme's preview in its views.

## Changed

- The config's texts have bounds: a name, as a font's family or a theme's, 256 characters at most, a path or an address 1024; a longer one is an error at its line.

## Fixed

- With the tab bar out of the titlebar and two tabs or more, the window's title stands centred and no longer lies over the plugins' buttons beside the window's buttons.
- A single tab's title in the titlebar is the window's title, in the system title's look, and dims as the window loses the focus.
- Choosing in a font picker the family that the config names already, after previewing another, shows it again.
- A file of the themes' folder that is no regular file, or of more than 1 MiB, or an `.itermcolors` file whose property list nests or repeats its values past what a color preset holds, is refused as a theme that does not read, where it could hold Lokyr or make it quit.

## Before you update

- Plugins installed for 1.0.0 keep working: the plugin API is 0.1.3, which takes 0.1.2's plugins.
