# The licenses' texts

The texts of the licenses that `deny.toml` allows, and of the LLVM exception, as SPDX's [license list](https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data) gives them, of its release v3.29.0, `text/<id>.txt`, unchanged. `cargo xtask bundle` takes them for a crate whose package carries no file of its license, its copyright's line made of its authors, so that every crate of the binary has its notice in the bundle's `Contents/Resources/licenses/crates`. A license that `deny.toml` comes to allow needs its text here; a test fails without it.

The texts of the licenses of the themes that the bundle holds, of the iTerm2-Color-Schemes collection, come from the same release: `cargo xtask themes` and `cargo xtask bundle` copy those of the held themes into `Contents/Resources/licenses/themes/licenses`, with `themes/<name>.txt`, the notice of each theme whose license was checked at its source, as its repository has it; `themes/copyrights.toml` gives each held theme's source's copyright line and author, which the notices' list and the themes' files take.
